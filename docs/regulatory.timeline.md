# Regulatory timeline (status at 23 November 2026)

> The Digital Omnibus on AI, **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744**, was published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on 27 July 2026. The dates below combine the original Art. 113 schedule with the Omnibus changes as reported by law-firm and regulatory commentary. **Verify against EUR-Lex before relying on a date.**

| Date | Milestone | Relevance to SmartAdvisor AI |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Aug 2024 | AI Act enters into force | Starting point |
| 2 Feb 2025 | Chapters I and II apply: definitions, prohibited practices (Art. 5), AI literacy (Art. 4) | Screening for prohibited practices and literacy measures already due |
| 2 Aug 2025 | General-purpose AI obligations, governance, penalties framework | LLM vendor obligations (Arts. 53-55); flows down to M1 and M3 |
| 27 Jul 2026 | Omnibus in force | Re-baselines high-risk dates; recasts Art. 4 as an obligation to *support* AI literacy |
| 2 Aug 2026 | General application, including **Art. 50 transparency** | M1 must disclose AI interaction; synthetic voice marking (Art. 50(2)) |
| 2 Dec 2026 | Art. 50(2) marking grace period ends for generative systems already on the market; two new prohibitions apply | M1 synthetic voice and any generated content |
| **2 Dec 2027** | **Annex III high-risk obligations apply** (was 2 Aug 2026) | M4 in employment, education, credit, insurance and similar uses |
| **2 Aug 2028** | **Annex I high-risk obligations apply** (was 2 Aug 2027) | M2 via the medical-device route |

## Planning consequences

1. The delay buys time, not relief: the obligations are unchanged and notified-body capacity and harmonised standards remain uncertain. Plan to be ready early.
2. Art. 50 is **already live**. It is the first compliance deliverable.
3. The classification decision in Assignment 2 sets which date applies, so it is the critical-path decision of the programme.
4. Re-baseline the roadmap on any change to the dates or to the harmonised standards.
