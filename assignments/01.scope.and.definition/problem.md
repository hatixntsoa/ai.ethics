## 1. Brief

You are the CTO of a digital services company that will design and deploy **SmartAdvisor AI**. The application combines a text and voice conversational agent, an image-analysis module that interprets situations or supplies diagnoses, a document-analysis system for contracts and invoices, and an automated recommendation engine that assists users in their decisions. It is used by both businesses and individuals, so uses and impact levels vary.

Before any compliance work can start, the board needs a defensible answer to one question: **does the AI Act apply to this product, and in what capacity?**

## 2. Questions

1. **Definition.** State the legal definition of an *AI system* under the Regulation and break it into testable elements.
2. **Qualification.** Justify, element by element, why SmartAdvisor AI (and each module) meets that definition.
3. **Scope.** Confirm the territorial and material scope (Art. 2) and identify any exclusion that could apply.
4. **Roles.** Qualify the company, its customers and its suppliers (provider, deployer, general-purpose AI model provider).
5. **Technical decomposition.** For each module, identify the inputs (text, audio, images, documents), the outputs (answers, recommendations), the level of autonomy, and the ML, NLP and computer-vision concepts involved.
6. **Literacy.** Identify the AI literacy obligation (Art. 4) and the measures it implies.
