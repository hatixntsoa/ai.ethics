## 1. Brief

Assignment 2 placed **M2 (diagnostic)** and **M4 (Annex III uses)** in the high-risk tier. The board has approved the programme and asks for a plan that is **technically implementable, auditable and costed in terms of effort, performance and user experience**.

The course exercise splits this into two parts, both covered here: (a) the requirements for high-risk systems, with concrete implementation elements; (b) the complementary analysis of the **technical and organisational implications** of compliance.

## 2. Questions

### Part A: Requirements for high-risk systems

1. Detail the measures required for **risk management, data quality, technical documentation, transparency, human oversight, and security and robustness**, with concrete technical implementation in SmartAdvisor AI.
2. Add record-keeping, the provider's quality management system, conformity assessment, declaration, CE marking, registration, post-market monitoring and incident reporting.
3. Explain what changes for the customers (deployers), including the fundamental-rights impact assessment.

### Part B: Implications

4. Describe the **architecture adaptations** for a stack of backend APIs, language models and computer-vision systems.
5. Assess **traceability, explainability and versioning**, including their effect on **cost, performance and user experience**.
6. Produce a **roadmap** consistent with the current legal calendar.
