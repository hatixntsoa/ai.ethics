> **Numbering note.** The course exercise labels this part "Chapter II" and asks for the four risk tiers. In the Regulation, Chapter II contains only the prohibited practices. The classification of *high-risk* systems sits in Chapter III Section 1 (Art. 6 and Annexes I and III), and transparency obligations in Chapter IV. The solution follows the exercise's logic and cites the exact legal anchor for each tier.

## 1. Brief

The board accepts that the AI Act applies. It now needs to know **how risky each module is in the regulator's eyes**, because the tier determines the obligations, the budget, the dates and the go-to-market plan. Pay particular attention to systems whose outputs can influence **important decisions in sensitive domains**.

## 2. Questions

1. Recall the four risk categories (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal) and their legal anchors.
2. Screen SmartAdvisor AI against every prohibited practice of Art. 5.
3. Classify each module (M1 to M4), justifying the choice by the **potential impact on users**.
4. Identify any high-risk system, and distinguish the **Annex I (product safety)** route from the **Annex III (use case)** route.
5. State how the *intended purpose* and *reasonably foreseeable misuse* change the answer, and propose how to control that.
6. Build a **risk register** that scores both organisational cyber risk and risk to persons, with treatments and residual ratings.
