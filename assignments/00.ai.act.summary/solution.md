## Summary of the [AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ai-act-explorer) chapters

### [CHAPTER I](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/1/): General provisions

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** this chapter sets out the subject matter of the regulation, its scope and the fundamental definitions.
- **Concerned parties:** applies broadly to the whole AI ecosystem, defining the framework for providers, deployers and other market players.

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **AI literacy ([Art. 4](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/4/)):** organisations must take measures to ensure a sufficient level of AI literacy among their staff and the people involved in operating AI systems.

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Set up AI **training** and awareness **programmes** for employees.
- Check whether the systems in use fall within the **scope** defined in [Article 2](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/2/).

🔑 **Key definitions**

- **AI system:** the central definition of the regulation ([Art. 3](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/3/)).
- **AI literacy:** the ability to understand and use AI in an informed way.

### [CHAPTER II](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/2/): Prohibited AI practices

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** this chapter lists the types of AI considered unacceptable because of the risks they pose to the values and security of the EU.

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **Prohibitions ([Art. 5](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/)):** it is **forbidden** to place on the market or use AI systems that engage in, among other things, cognitive manipulation, social scoring, or real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces (subject to strict exceptions).

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Audit existing AI systems to make sure none falls under the **prohibited practices** of [Article 5](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/5/).

### [CHAPTER III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/3/): High-risk AI systems

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** the core of the regulation, dealing with systems that present significant risks (health, safety, fundamental rights).
- **Concerned parties:** mainly **providers**, but also **deployers**, importers and distributors.

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **Risk management system ([Art. 9](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/9/)):** an iterative system must be established and maintained throughout the lifecycle of the AI system.
- **Data governance ([Art. 10](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/10/)):** training datasets must meet high quality criteria.
- **Technical documentation ([Art. 11](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/11/)):** must be drawn up before placing on the market to demonstrate compliance.
- **Human oversight ([Art. 14](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/14/)):** systems must be designed to allow effective oversight by natural persons.
- **Provider obligations ([Art. 16](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/16/)):** providers **must** ensure compliance, affix the CE marking and put a quality management system in place.
- **Fundamental rights impact assessment ([Art. 27](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/27/)):** mandatory for certain deployers before use.

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Establish a **Quality Management System ([Art. 17](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/17/))**.
- Draw up the **Technical Documentation** according to [Annex IV](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/4/).
- Carry out a **Conformity Assessment ([Art. 43](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/43/))**.
- Sign the **EU Declaration of Conformity ([Art. 47](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/47/))** and affix the **CE marking ([Art. 48](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/48/))**.
- Register in the **EU database ([Art. 49](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/49/))**.

⚠️ **Risks and penalties**

- Non-compliant systems may be subject to **corrective measures** or withdrawal from the market.

### [CHAPTER IV](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/4/): Transparency obligations for certain systems

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** applies to AI systems that interact with humans or generate content (e.g. chatbots, deepfakes).

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **User information ([Art. 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/)):** providers **must** ensure that users know they are interacting with an AI. AI-generated content (deepfakes) must be marked as such.

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Put clear **information notices** in user interfaces.
- Integrate **marking (watermarking)** or detection systems for generated content.

### [CHAPTER V](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/5/): General-purpose AI (GPAI) models

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** regulates foundation models such as Large Language Models (LLMs).
- **Concerned parties:** providers of general-purpose AI models.

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **Documentation and transparency ([Art. 53](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/53/)):** providers **must** supply technical documentation and information to downstream providers who integrate the model.
- **Systemic-risk models (Arts. [51](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/51/) and [55](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/55/)):** reinforced obligations (model evaluations, adversarial testing, reporting of serious incidents) for the most powerful models.

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Prepare the technical documentation according to Annexes [XI](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/11/) and [XII](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/12/).
- Appoint an **authorised representative** in the EU if the provider is established outside the EU ([Art. 54](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/54/)).
- Follow the **Code of Practice** to demonstrate compliance ([Art. 56](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/56/)).

### [CHAPTER VI](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/6/): Measures in support of innovation

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** to facilitate the development of AI through safe frameworks.

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **Regulatory sandboxes ([Art. 57](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/57/)):** Member States **must** establish at least one sandbox by August 2026 to allow testing under supervision.

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- For SMEs and start-ups: find out about the **specific support measures ([Art. 62](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/62/))** that reduce compliance costs.

### [CHAPTER VII](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/7/): Governance

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** administrative organisation at European and national levels.
- **Key actors:** the AI Office, the European Artificial Intelligence Board, the Scientific Panel.

### [CHAPTER VIII](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/8/): EU database for high-risk systems

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** creation of a centralised public register for the high-risk AI systems listed in [Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/).

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Register the AI system in the database before placing it on the market ([Art. 71](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/71/)).

### [CHAPTER IX](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/9/): Post-market monitoring and enforcement

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** continuous monitoring of the performance and safety of systems after deployment.

🎯 **Key obligations and requirements**

- **Monitoring plan ([Art. 72](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/72/)):** providers **must** implement a post-market monitoring system.
- **Incident reporting ([Art. 73](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/73/)):** obligation to report **serious incidents** to the supervisory authorities.

⚠️ **Risks and penalties**

- **Right to explanation ([Art. 86](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/86/)):** individuals have the right to obtain an explanation of decisions made about them by a high-risk AI system.

### [CHAPTER X](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/10/): Codes of conduct and guidelines

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** to encourage the voluntary application of the high-risk requirements to other AI systems ([Art. 95](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/95/)).

### [CHAPTER XII](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/12/): Penalties

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** definition of the fines regime in case of non-compliance.

⚠️ **Risks and penalties (Arts. [99](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/99/) and [101](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/101/))**

- **Administrative fines:** penalties can be very heavy (the specific amounts depend on the severity and on the size of the company).
- Specific fines apply to providers of GPAI models.

### [CHAPTER XIII](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/chapter/13/): Final provisions

📌 **Title and scope**

- **Purpose:** implementation timetable and amendments to other legislation.

✅ **Compliance checklist**

- Monitor the dates of the phased entry into application ([Art. 113](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/)).
- Check the status of AI systems already on the market ([Art. 111](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/111/)).
