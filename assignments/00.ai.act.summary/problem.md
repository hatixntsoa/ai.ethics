# Assignment 0: Summary of the EU AI Act

| | |
|---|---|
| Course | AI Ethics, M1 OCC 2026 |
| Regulation | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) |
| Type | Preliminary assignment, individual |
| Language of the submission | French |

## 1. Brief

Produce a structured **summary of the EU AI Act**, chapter by chapter, so that a reader can quickly see what each chapter regulates, who it concerns, what it requires and what a compliance team should check.

## 2. Expected structure (per chapter)

| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Title and scope | Purpose of the chapter and the actors concerned |
| Key obligations and requirements | The articles that matter, in one line each |
| Compliance checklist | Concrete actions an organisation should take |
| Key definitions | Where the chapter introduces them |
| Risks and sanctions | Where the chapter attaches consequences |

## 3. Why it comes first

The summary is the reading map for the rest of the repository. The three case-study assignments that follow apply Chapters I, II and III to the SmartAdvisor AI scenario:

1. [Scope and definition](../01.scope.and.definition/problem.md)
2. [Risk classification](../02.risk.classification/problem.md)
3. [High-risk requirements](../03.high.risk.requirements/problem.md)

## 4. Deliverable

[solution.md](solution.md): the summary as submitted (in French), followed by an update note on what changed in the law since it was written.
